Delete your ilem account

You can delete your ilem account, and the data linked to it, at any time. ilem is made by Ilem Ltd.

In the app

  1. Open ilem and go to Profile, then Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. You'll be asked to sign in again to confirm. Once you confirm, your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can't open the app, email hello@ilemapp.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record: your name, email address and user ID.
  • Your free picks, favourites and the link between your account and your subscription.
  • Any lectures downloaded for offline listening.

Notes, progress, bookmarks and books on your bookshelf are stored only on your phone. Delete the app to remove them.

What is kept

  • Records of purchases are kept by Apple, Google and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, for as long as the law requires for payment records.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an active ilem Plus subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, ideally before deleting your account.

For more, see our Privacy policy.
