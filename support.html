Support

Answers to the common questions. If yours isn't here, email hello@ilemapp.com and we'll reply within two working days.

How do I cancel ilem Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, so you cancel in your store settings. In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Manage subscription. Or on iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep ilem Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, because they take the payment. For the App Store, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, use the refund option in your Play order history. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

I reinstalled the app or changed phone and lost ilem Plus

Sign in with the same account, then go to Profile, then Settings, then Restore purchases. Your subscription is linked to the Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

What are free picks?

When you sign in you get a few free picks. Each one permanently unlocks the study tools (transcript, summary, notes, quiz and revision) on a lecture or daily benefit of your choice, without paying. The app tells you how many you have before you use one.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. You'll be asked to sign in again to confirm. This removes your account and unlinks your subscription. It doesn't cancel the subscription itself, so cancel that in your store settings first. Notes and progress are stored on your phone; delete the app to remove them.

Prayer times are different from my mosque

Mosques use different calculation methods. Tap the prayer times card on the home screen and choose another method, such as Moonsighting Committee for much of the UK, or change the Asr setting. Times are calculated on your phone from your location, which never leaves the device.

Audio won't play or download

Lectures stream over the internet, so check your connection first. If a download stalls, delete it from My Library and try again on Wi-Fi. If it still fails, email us the lecture title and your phone model.

I found a mistake in a transcript or translation

Please tell us. Email the lecture or benefit title and roughly where the mistake is, and we'll correct it in a future update.
